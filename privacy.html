Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Focus Frame does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No data collection. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
  • No network access. The app never connects to the internet.
  • No screen contents. To place the frame around a window, the app reads only the position and size of windows and which app owns them, through standard macOS APIs. It never reads, records, or captures what is shown in any window, and it does not read window titles.
  • Settings stay on your Mac. Your preferences (dim strength, border, Esc behavior, and whether to open at login) are saved locally by macOS and never leave your Mac.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
